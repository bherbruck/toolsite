🏷️ Inventory

This app has no screens. Its data is a set of tables with row-level policies: each person sees the stock of their own warehouse and nothing else.

To use it, connect your AI assistant to this site's /me/mcp and ask it to:

  • list your apps (my_apps), then
  • query my_stock, my_movements or stock_totals.

Two people who run the same query get different rows.
